Lesson 6

Writing a Cohesive Paragraph

Paragraph
Sentences organized around one topic.
Claim
A statement of the main idea to prove or support.
Evidence
Facts, examples, statistics, quotes, or data supporting a claim.
Cohesive paragraph
Sentences connected clearly around one topic.
5–6 sentence paragraph
A practice paragraph with 1 topic, 1 claim, 3 evidences, and 1 conclusion.
Conclusion
The ending that wraps up the paragraph's idea.
Lessons 7–8

Avoiding Plagiarism

Plagiarism
Presenting someone else's work as your own.
Intellectual theft
Taking another person's ideas or work without proper credit.
Academic dishonesty
Unethical behavior involving school work or academic credit.
Paraphrasing
Rewriting an author's ideas in your own words while keeping the meaning.
Summarizing
Shortening an author's main ideas using your own words.
Referencing
Acknowledging the writer and showing where ideas came from.
In-text reference
Source information placed in the essay or paragraph.
Reference list
Sources listed at the end of the essay or report.
APA
American Psychological Association citation format.
Common knowledge
Information widely known that normally does not need a reference.
Lesson 10

Narrative Essay

Narrative
A story or account of events, experiences, or interactions.
Setting
The time, place, atmosphere, and mood of a story.
Characters
The individuals in a story.
Plot
The sequence of events with a beginning, middle, and end.
Conflict
The problem or struggle that needs to be solved.
Resolution
How the problem is solved.
Point of view
Who tells the story or how it is told.
Theme
The big idea about human nature or life.
Literary device
A writing technique used to create a particular effect.
Simile
Comparison using “like” or “as.”
Metaphor
Saying one thing is another to show similarity.
Personification
Giving human traits to objects or ideas.
Hyperbole
Exaggeration used for emphasis.
Onomatopoeia
A word written to imitate a sound.
Lesson 12

Opinion Essay

Fact
A statement that can be proven true or false.
Opinion
A belief, feeling, or thought based on personal perspective.
Opinion essay
An essay that expresses a position about an issue.
Claim / main idea
The position of agreeing, disagreeing, or proposing a solution.
Evidence in an opinion essay
Reasons supporting the writer's position.
Introduction
Presents the main claim or position.
Body
Explains reasons supporting the opinion.
Conclusion
Gives a summary, final thought, or call to action.
Lesson 13

Persuasive Essay

Persuasive / argumentative essay
A debate on paper that tries to convince the reader.
Pick a side
Choose a position or suggest a solution.
Audience
The people you are writing to persuade.
Logic
Reasoning used to support an argument.
Feelings
Emotional appeals used to persuade.
Trustworthy sources
Reliable sources used for research.
Both sides
Considering the opposing argument before responding to it.
Counterargument
The other side of an argument.
